# Public Policy In-House Delivery & Bureaucracy Offset Skill

Use this skill whenever the user wants to design a practical, costed public policy proposal that replaces outsourced private contracts with publicly owned equipment and in-house delivery crews—funded by trimming or transitioning duplicated administrative/executive roles.

## Core Methodology (5-Step Workflow)

### Step 1: Establish the Baseline Problem & Service Target
* Identify the current delivery model (e.g., outsourced contractors, fragmented local ownership) and quantify the service backlog using official audit or government reports (e.g., Auditor-General reports, departmental annual reports).
* Define a clear annual operational target (e.g., 500,000 potholes patched/year, X inspections/year, Y km of network maintained).

### Step 2: Calculate Fleet / Equipment Capacity
* Determine the realistic daily output of one equipment unit/crew under local working conditions (accounting for travel/deadhead time).
* Multiply daily output by realistic annual workable days (accounting for weather, maintenance, and transit—e.g., ~190 workable dry days/year for road works).
* Calculate the exact fleet size required to meet the annual target, leaving a buffer above the minimum threshold.
* Define clear **scope boundaries** (what the rapid-response fleet solves vs. what must be escalated to heavy structural teams).

### Step 3: Apply Bulk State Procurement Leverage ("Massive Negotiation")
* Benchmark single-unit retail costs paid by small buyers (e.g., local councils).
* Apply realistic state-level bulk tender discounts (Best and Final Offer / BAFO):
  * **Capital Discount (~15%–20%):** Volume discount on bulk fleet purchase.
  * **Consumables & Servicing Discount (~10%–15%):** Multi-year locked pricing for materials, spare parts, and preventative maintenance.
  * **Local Content / Fit-Out Clause:** Require local assembly/servicing hubs to secure regional jobs and parts supply.

### Step 4: Build the Budget-Neutral Bureaucracy Offset Table
* Calculate full employer on-costs for public service roles (base salary + ~18% for superannuation, payroll tax, and workers' compensation).
* Separate **Year 1 Costs** (One-Off Capital Purchase + Annual Operating Costs) from **Year 2+ Costs** (Annual Operating Costs only).
* Present a clear side-by-side offset table showing how many **Mid-Level Admin roles** OR **Senior Executive roles** equal the exact capital and operating expenditure.
* Highlight the **Year 2+ Structural Dividend** (the recurring annual budget freed up once capital equipment is paid off in Year 1).

### Step 5: Design the Workforce Transition & Retraining Pathway
To avoid high upfront statutory redundancy payouts ($60k–$100k per permanent employee) and maintain political/union viability, structure workforce reductions in four tiers:
1. **Cut External Consultants & Labour-Hire First:** Immediate cash savings with zero redundancy payout liability.
2. **Merge Duplicated Corporate Units:** Consolidate overlapping HR, communications, policy, legal, and IT teams across sub-agencies.
3. **Voluntary Frontline Retraining Scheme:** Offer displaced back-office staff state-funded licensing, accreditation, and placement into frontline operational roles (e.g., heavy vehicle drivers, traffic controllers, field inspectors/auditors).
4. **Salary Maintenance & Relocation Bridge:** Include a 6-month wage-protection bridge and regional relocation grant to encourage voluntary uptake.

## Output Formatting Standards
Every policy document generated using this skill must include:
1. **Executive Summary** (3–4 sentences stating fleet size, annual output, Year 1 budget neutrality, and Year 2+ savings).
2. **Problem Statement** (grounded in official audit/departmental metrics).
3. **Operational Blueprint** (capacity maths, regional depot deployment, and technical scope limits).
4. **Bulk Procurement Strategy** (retail vs. bulk-negotiated pricing).
5. **Complete Financial & Staffing Maths Table** (Baseline assumptions + Year 1 & Year 2+ offset calculations).
6. **Workforce Transition & Retraining Plan** (4-step hierarchy).
7. **12-Month Implementation Roadmap** (broken into quarterly/bi-monthly milestones).
8. **Source References & Data Verification Appendix** (numbered list of primary government reports, procurement guides, and industry benchmarks so every figure can be independently audited).
