# Public Policy In-House Delivery & Bureaucracy Offset Skill (v2.0 — Autonomous Research & Self-Healing Architecture)

Use this skill whenever the user wants to design a practical, costed, and audit-proof public policy proposal that replaces outsourced private contracts with publicly owned equipment and in-house delivery crews—funded by trimming or transitioning duplicated administrative/executive roles.

---

## Phase 1: Mandatory Primary Research Protocol (Zero-Guesswork Rule)

Before drafting any policy text or financial table, you **MUST** execute a structured multi-query search to gather live, jurisdiction-specific primary data. Never rely on static training assumptions when real audit figures exist.

### 1.1 Required Research Queries
1. **Service Backlog & Audit Data:** Search `[Jurisdiction] Auditor-General report [service/infrastructure] maintenance backlog condition` (e.g., VAGO reports, ANAO reports).
2. **Departmental Headcount & Budget:** Search `[Department Name] Annual Report [latest year] FTE staff numbers budget expenditure`.
3. **Public Sector Salary & Executive Benchmarks:** Search `[Jurisdiction] Public Service enterprise agreement salary bands` and `Senior Executive Service remuneration`.
4. **Equipment Retail & Operating Costs:** Search `[Equipment Name] municipal council purchase price Australia` and `daily output capacity`.
5. **Procurement & Legal Rules:** Search `[Jurisdiction] Government Purchasing Board negotiation guide BAFO` and `[Jurisdiction] OH&S traffic/safety requirements [operation]`.

### 1.2 Source Hierarchy & Verification Standard
* **Tier 1 (Mandatory for Core Claims):** Auditor-General reports, Parliamentary Tabled Papers, Departmental Annual Reports, Official Budget Papers, and Statutory Procurement Guides.
* **Tier 2 (Acceptable for Industry Benchmarks):** Local council procurement disclosures, manufacturer technical specifications, and registered union/industry workforce submissions.
* **Prohibited:** Unattributed media commentary, generic estimates without local validation, or dead/fabricated URLs. Every number in the final policy must map directly to a numbered entry in the **Source References & Data Verification Appendix**.

## Phase 2: Core Policy Engineering Workflow (5-Step Model)

### Step 1: Establish the Baseline Problem & Service Target
* Quantify the asset/service backlog using Tier 1 audit figures.
* Identify the structural failure of the outsourced model (contractor mark-ups, multi-tier subcontracting, loss of in-house engineering oversight).
* Lock in a concrete annual operational target (e.g., 500,000 potholes patched/year).

### Step 2: Calculate Fleet / Equipment Capacity
* Determine realistic daily output per unit under local conditions (factoring in regional travel/deadhead time).
* Multiply daily output by realistic annual workable days (accounting for weather, scheduled servicing, and transit—e.g., ~190 workable dry days/year for civil works).
* Size the fleet with a **5%–15% operational buffer** above the minimum required units.
* Define explicit **technical scope boundaries** (what the rapid-response fleet solves vs. what is logged via GPS and escalated to heavy structural teams).

### Step 3: Apply Bulk State Procurement Leverage ("Massive Negotiation")
* Benchmark single-unit retail prices paid by small buyers (e.g., individual local councils).
* Structure a competitive **Best and Final Offer (BAFO)** state tender:
  * **Capital Volume Discount (~15%–20%):** Bulk fleet purchase price reduction.
  * **Bundled Consumables & Servicing (~10%–15%):** Multi-year locked pricing for raw materials, spare parts, and preventative maintenance.
  * **Local Fit-Out & Regional Servicing Clause:** Mandate in-state body assembly and regional servicing hubs to guarantee parts availability and regional employment.

### Step 4: Build the Budget-Neutral Bureaucracy Offset Table
* Compute full employer on-costs for public service roles: `Base Salary × 1.18` (accounting for superannuation guarantee, payroll tax, and workers' compensation).
* Separate **Year 1 Total Expenditure** (`One-Off Capital + Annual Operating Costs`) from **Year 2+ Ongoing Expenditure** (`Annual Operating Costs only`).
* Calculate exact role offsets for both **Mid-Level Admin Roles** and **Senior Executive Roles**.
* Quantify the **Year 2+ Structural Dividend** (the recurring annual budget freed once capital equipment is paid off in Year 1).

### Step 5: Design the 4-Tier Workforce Transition & Retraining Pathway
To prevent upfront statutory redundancy payouts ($60,000–$100,000 per permanent employee) from eroding Year 1 cash savings, sequence workforce reductions in strict order:
1. **Terminate External Consultants & Labour-Hire First:** Instant cash savings with zero redundancy liability.
2. **Consolidate Duplicated Corporate Units:** Merge overlapping HR, media, policy, legal, and IT teams across sub-agencies.
3. **Voluntary Frontline Retraining Scheme:** Offer displaced permanent staff state-funded licensing, accreditation, and placement into frontline roles (e.g., heavy vehicle operators, traffic control escorts, field inspectors/contract auditors).
4. **Salary Maintenance & Relocation Bridge:** Provide a 6-month wage-protection bridge and regional relocation grant to maximise voluntary uptake.

## Phase 3: Self-Healing Verification & Stress-Test Loop

Before outputting the final document, execute an internal **4-Gate Self-Healing Audit**. If any gate fails, automatically recalculate or revise the draft before presenting it to the user:

* **Gate 1 — Mathematical & Python Integrity Check:** Run all arithmetic (`Fleet × Unit Cost`, `Annual Ops ÷ Admin Cost`, `Year 2+ Dividend`) in a Python interpreter. Verify zero rounding contradictions across prose, bullet points, and markdown tables.
* **Gate 2 — Regulatory & OH&S Reality Check:** Verify whether local safety laws require ancillary crew/equipment (e.g., mandatory traffic control escort vehicles for live-lane road works). If omitted, inject those costs into the annual operating budget.
* **Gate 3 — Redundancy Cash-Flow Trap Check:** Check if Year 1 relies on firing permanent staff who require upfront exit payouts. If so, ensure external contractor cuts and voluntary retraining offsets cover Year 1 cash flow so the policy remains genuinely budget-neutral on Day 1.
* **Gate 4 — Citation & Link Audit:** Confirm every statistic in the text has a matching numbered source in Section 7 with a verified URL and publication date. Remove or qualify any claim lacking a verified source.

## Phase 4: Self-Improving AI Loop (Post-Generation Calibration)

At the end of every policy generation run, perform a silent or appended **Policy Calibration & Retrospective** to upgrade future runs:

1. **Sensitivity Stress-Test:** Automatically check how the model behaves if bulk procurement discounts achieve only **50% of target** (e.g., 9% instead of 18%) or if workable weather days drop by **15%**. Note the contingency buffer in the brief.
2. **Reusable Parameter Extraction:** Abstract the jurisdiction-specific constants (e.g., `VPS_ON_COST_MULTIPLIER = 1.18`, `WORKABLE_DAYS = 190`, `BAFO_CAPITAL_DISCOUNT = 0.18`) so the template can be rapidly cloned to adjacent portfolios (e.g., heavy road rehabilitation, public housing maintenance, regional rail, parks/forestry).
3. **User Feedback Integration:** When the user provides new constraints, political angles, or local data, update the baseline variables and increment the policy version (`v2`, `v3`) while preserving full audit traceability.

## Mandatory Output Structure

Every policy document generated by this skill must follow this exact 8-section architecture:
1. **Executive Summary** (Fleet size, annual output, Year 1 budget neutrality, Year 2+ recurring dividend).
2. **The Problem: Why the Current Model Fails** (Audit metrics, contractor mark-ups, bureaucratic duplication).
3. **Operational Blueprint** (Daily/annual capacity maths, regional depot placement, technical scope boundaries).
4. **Bulk Procurement Strategy** (Retail vs. BAFO negotiated pricing and local content clauses).
5. **Complete Financial & Staffing Maths** (Baseline assumptions + Year 1 & Year 2+ offset table).
6. **Workforce Transition: Back-Office to Frontline Retraining** (4-step hierarchy).
7. **12-Month Implementation Roadmap** (Bi-monthly execution milestones).
8. **Source References & Data Verification Appendix** (Numbered primary sources with verified URLs and dates).
