# The Levers

**Eight cross-cutting system levers that make the portfolios work.**

A portfolio tells you *what* the state is trying to achieve. A lever tells you *how* the state can change the system. The levers are owned by no single minister, which is exactly why they need explicit accountability.

| Lever | The question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| [Procurement and market design](procurement-and-market-design/README.md) | How does the state buy, and what does it build? | The state's purchasing power is its largest untapped policy instrument. |
| [Digital, data and AI](digital-data-and-ai/README.md) | How does the state know, decide and serve? | Common platforms and data are the difference between one government and 306 silos. |
| [Workforce and capability](workforce-and-capability/README.md) | Who delivers, and can they? | The binding constraint on almost every policy is people. |
| [Regulation and red tape](regulation-and-red-tape/README.md) | What rules shape behaviour, and what do they cost? | Bad regulation is a tax on citizens and business; good regulation is a public good. |
| [Intergovernmental and federalism](intergovernmental-and-federalism/README.md) | What does the federation allow? | Many of Victoria's biggest levers are shared with the Commonwealth. |
| [Integrity and trust](integrity-and-trust/README.md) | Can citizens believe it? | Without trust, reform is dismissed and institutions fail. |
| [Democracy, transparency and the operator culture](democracy-transparency-and-operator-culture/README.md) | Who decided, on what evidence, and who influenced it? | Replace the salesman politician with the civil-servant operator: show the ledger, sit for the examination. |
| [Cost of living and household budgets](cost-of-living-and-household-budgets/README.md) | What does a household actually pay and receive? | The citizen experiences government as a budget, not a portfolio. |

---

## How to use the levers

1. **Read the lever before the portfolio.** It tells you what is possible across the system.
2. **Name the lever in every portfolio README.** A portfolio that does not use procurement, digital, workforce or regulation is probably avoiding the hard part.
3. **Assign a lever owner.** A lever without an owner is a conference theme.
4. **Measure the lever.** Procurement by outcome, digital by reuse, workforce by pipeline, regulation by burden, federalism by outcomes, integrity by trust, democracy by public decision records, cost of living by household impact.

## The lever test

A lever is working when:

- It is used deliberately, not accidentally.
- It has a named owner and a public metric.
- It reduces duplication or increases capability.
- It is visible to the citizen.
- It survives a change of minister.
