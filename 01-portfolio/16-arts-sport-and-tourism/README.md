# 16 — Arts, Sport and Tourism

> **Citizen promise:** A creative state that pays its way.
>
> **Operator thesis:** Victoria's arts, sport and tourism agencies are good at winning events and funding institutions, but weaker at building **industries, participation and regional spread**. The next move is to treat the creative economy as an industry, events as customer acquisition, and community sport as public health infrastructure — with a hard test on every dollar.
>
> **2026 baseline:** Victoria's creative economy is valued at about **$41.2 billion**. The 2026–27 Budget includes **$81.1 million** in new creative-industry initiatives, **$92 million** for the State Sport Centres Trust and Kardinia Park Stadium Trust, and **$25 million** for community sport participation and facilities. [A]

## 1. The outcome

- **Outcome 1:** A thriving creative economy with jobs and exports.
- **Outcome 2:** Victorians participate in sport, culture and community life.
- **Outcome 3:** Major events deliver a measurable return.
- **Outcome 4:** Regional Victoria shares the cultural and visitor economy.
- **Outcome 5:** Cultural and sporting infrastructure is well maintained and used.

**What success looks like by 2030:** Creative employment and exports grow; participation rises; major events deliver a positive net return; regional visitation and cultural activity grow; and infrastructure is fit for purpose.

**What failure looks like:** More events, more grants and more institutions, but the same concentration in Melbourne and the same maintenance backlog.

**What the citizen experiences:** Great major events, but patchy local cultural and sporting facilities; expensive tickets; limited regional touring; and unclear return on public spending.
**What the frontline experiences:** Short funding cycles, application burden, infrastructure maintenance gaps and event-driven workload.

---

## 3. Where the money and time leak

- **Event subsidy:** Major events are costly, and the return is often poorly measured.
- **Institutional concentration:** Funding concentrates in Melbourne's major institutions.
- **Regional access:** Touring, screen and sport infrastructure are uneven.
- **Maintenance backlog:** Cultural and sporting assets are not consistently funded for renewal.
- **Fragmented agencies:** Creative Victoria, Visit Victoria, Sport and Recreation Victoria and event bodies duplicate marketing, data and administration.
- **Workforce:** Creative and tourism workers face insecure work and skills gaps.
- **Evaluation:** Economic impact claims are often gross, not net, and rarely independently tested.
- **Community sport:** Facility gaps and rising costs reduce participation.

**Estimated annual value of the leak:** To be costed. The largest items are event subsidies, maintenance backlogs and duplicated marketing. [D]

---

## 4. The reframe

**The old frame:** Arts, sport and tourism are subsidised amenity, and major events are prestige.

**The new frame:** The creative economy is an **industry**, community sport is **public health infrastructure**, and major events are **customer acquisition** with a measurable return.

**Three alternative models:**

| Model | How it works | Upside | Downside | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. More grants and events** | Expand programs and event calendar | Visible | Weak return and concentration | Not sufficient |
| **B. Industry and participation system** | Creative industry investment, regional touring, community sport, event evaluation | Jobs, participation, regional spread | Requires discipline and data | Preferred base |
| **C. Cultural and sporting infrastructure authority** | Consolidate asset ownership and maintenance | Whole-of-life asset management | Centralisation risk | Strong complement |

**The reframe that matters most:** **Measure net return, not gross impact.** Every event, grant and institution should report net economic, social and cultural return, including the public cost.

**Supporting moves:**

- **Creative industry investment:** screen, games, music, design and digital content, with export and jobs targets.
- **Regional touring guarantee:** every region gets a minimum cultural program.
- **Community sport infrastructure:** fund the facilities that unlock participation.
- **Event evaluation framework:** independent, net-return assessment before renewal.
- **Tourism workforce and dispersal:** skills, housing and regional itineraries.
- **Asset stewardship:** a whole-of-life plan for cultural and sporting infrastructure.

## 5. Cut here

| Cut / stop / move | Current owner | What happens to the people | Saving or capacity released | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sunset events and grants that fail a net-return test | DJSIR/Visit Victoria | Reallocate to proven programs | Event and grant savings | [D] |
| Consolidate duplicate marketing and data functions | Creative Victoria/Visit Victoria/Sport | Shared services | Admin cost | [D] |
| Reduce low-value cultural and sporting grants | DJSIR | Reallocate to regional touring and facilities | Program savings | [D] |
| Stop funding infrastructure without a maintenance plan | DJSIR/trusts | Better asset management | Deferred maintenance | [D] |
| Consolidate small event bodies | DJSIR | Shared services | Governance cost | [D] |

**The hard calls:** Cutting events and grants will be loudly contested. It is justified only with transparent net-return evidence and a reallocation to participation and regional access.
**What we will not cut:** Major cultural institutions; community sport access; regional touring; First Peoples creative practice.

## 6. Add here

| Add / build / buy / guarantee | Why | Who delivers | Year 1 cost | Year 2+ cost | Offset |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Creative industry investment fund | Jobs and exports | Creative Victoria | Moderate | Operating | Low-value grants |
| Regional touring guarantee | Access and regional economies | Creative Victoria/venues | Moderate | Operating | Event subsidy |
| Community sport facility fund | Participation and health | Sport and Recreation Victoria/councils | Capital | Operating | Health costs |
| Event net-return framework | Accountability | DJSIR/Visit Victoria | Low | Operating | Existing evaluation |
| Tourism workforce and dispersal plan | Skills and regional spread | Visit Victoria/DJSIR | Low | Operating | Marketing spend |
| Cultural and sporting asset plan | Whole-of-life management | Trusts/DJSIR | Capital | Operating | Deferred maintenance |
| First Peoples creative and sporting fund | Self-determination and culture | Creative Victoria/Aboriginal organisations | Moderate | Operating | Mainstream programs |

**The new capability:** The state must be able to **measure net return, invest in industry, deliver regional access and manage cultural assets**.

**The new deal:** The citizen gets culture, sport and events that are accessible and affordable. The artist and athlete get a career path. The state gets jobs, participation and a measurable return.

## 7. The ecosystem deal

| Party | What they give | What they get | What happens if they do not move |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commonwealth | National arts, screen and sport funding | Better outcomes | State fills gaps |
| Local government | Local venues, sport and events | Funding and partnership | Fragmentation |
| Arts organisations | Creativity and community | Stable funding | Precarity |
| Sports bodies | Participation and facilities | Investment | Decline |
| Event promoters | Content and audiences | Support and certainty | Events leave |
| Tourism operators | Product and workforce | Visitation | Regional gaps |
| Unions / workers | Skills and fair work | Good jobs | Workforce shortages |
| First Peoples | Cultural authority | Self-determination | Cultural loss |
| Audiences / participants | Engagement | Access and quality | Apathy |

**The convening mechanism:** A Creative, Sport and Visitor Economy Board with DJSIR, agencies, councils, industry, unions, First Peoples and regions.
**The accountability mechanism:** A public dashboard of jobs, participation, visitation, regional spread, event net return and asset condition.

## 8. Cross-portfolio joins

- **Regional Victoria** — touring, events and tourism dispersal.
- **Education and skills** — creative and tourism workforce.
- **Transport and roads** — event access and regional connectivity.
- **Housing and planning** — cultural and sporting infrastructure in growth areas.
- **Digital, data and AI** — screen, games and digital distribution.
- **Fiscal and public sector** — net-return evaluation and asset stewardship.

**The shared asset:** A single cultural, sporting and visitor economy dashboard.

## 11. Open questions and sources

**What we do not know:**

- The net return of major events.
- The creative economy's true employment and export contribution.
- The community sport facility gap.
- The regional touring cost and demand.
- The cultural and sporting asset maintenance backlog.

**What we will commission or test in the first year:**

- An event net-return evaluation.
- A creative workforce census.
- A community sport facility audit.
- A regional touring trial.
